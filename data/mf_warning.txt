2.C ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:
OVER THE LAST 18 HOURS, CONVECTION HAS BEEN FLUCTUATING NEAR THE
SYSTEM'S CENTRE, WITH A TEMPORARY INTENSIFICATION LAST NIGHT THEN
SOME WEAKENING THIS MORNING. THURSDAY EVENING'S 1809Z ASCAT-C PASS,
AS WELL AS RECENT SATELLITE AND MICROWAVE IMAGES (GOSAT-GW/2145Z,
F18/0045Z, WSFM/0220Z) SUGGEST THAT A SMALL VORTEX HAS FORMED IN THE
SOUTHERN PART OF A TROUGH AXIS. LAST NIGHT'S ASCAT WINDS WERE BETWEEN
20 AND 25 KT. WHILE THE LOW-LEVEL CIRCULATION SEEMS BETTER ORGANIZED
THAN YESTERDAY, CONVECTION IS STILL TOO WEAK AND NOT PERSISTENT
ENOUGH TO CLASSIFY THE SYSTEM AS A TROPICAL DISTURBANCE. IT IS
THEREFORE LEFT AT ZONE OF DISTURBED WEATHER STAGE FOR THE MOMENT.
MOREOVER, THIS MORNING'S 0515Z ASCAT-C PASS SHOWS WINDS NEAR 30 KT IN
THE SOUTHEASTERN PART OF THE CIRCULATION, BUT THIS DATA WAS AVAILABLE
TOO LATE TO BE TAKEN INTO ACCOUNT IN THE RSMC'S 06Z ANALYSIS. THE
ESTIMATED INTENSITY OF 25 KT WILL THEREFORE BE UPGRADED TO 30 KT IN
THE BEST-TRACK (WHILE KEEPING THE SYSTEM AS A ZONE OF DISTURBED
WEATHER).

IN TERMS OF TRACK, THE SYSTEM IS EXPECTED TO KEEP MOVING
SOUTHWESTWARD OVER THE NEXT 12 TO 18 HOURS, ALONG THE EDGE OF A
LOW-TO-MID-TROPOSPHERIC RIDGE TO THE SOUTHEAST. THE SYSTEM IS THEN
EXPECTED TO TURN WESTWARD OR WEST-NORTHWESTWARD UNDER THE INFLUENCE
OF A NEW SUBTROPICAL RIDGE SOUTH OF MADAGASCAR. MODEL SPREAD
INCREASES ON SUNDAY AS THE SYSTEM APPROACHES MADAGASCAR, AS THE TRACK
IS ALSO DEPENDENT OF THE INTENSITY FORECAST.

IN TERMS OF INTENSITY, ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS ARE SLIGHTLY
FAVORABLE, WITH SEA SURFACE WATERS AROUND 27C, GOOD UPPER DIVERGENCE
AND LOW VERTICAL WIND SHEAR UNTIL TONIGHT. HOWEVER, THE LACK OF
SURFACE CONVERGENCE SEAMS TO HINDER THE INTENSIFICATION IN THE SHORT
TERM. FROM SATURDAY, NORTHWESTERLY WIND SHEAR SHOULD INCREASE. IN THE
SAME TIME, THE SYSTEM IS EXPECTED TO MOVE OVER SLIGHTLY WARMER
SURFACE WATERS TO THE NORTH OF TROMELIN. CONSEQUENTLY THE SYSTEM HAS
A POSSIBILITY TO REACH TROPICAL STORM STAGE BETWEEN TONIGHT AND
TOMORROW. THE EFFECTS OF WIND SHEAR COULD INCREASE BY SUNDAY AS THE
SYSTEM ACCELERATES IN THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION OF WIND SHEAR, WHICH
COULD LEAD TO SOME SLIGHT WEAKENING. ON SUNDAY EVENING SHEAR SHOULD
DECREASE AGAIN AND LOW-LEVEL CONVERGENCE SHOULD INCREASE AS THE LOW
APPROACHES MADAGASCAR'S COAST. MODELS (ESPECIALLY ECMWF-IFS AND
AROME) THUS SUGGEST POSSIBLE REINTENSIFICATION JUST BEFORE LANDFALL
SUNDAY NIGHT. FROM MONDAY ONWARDS, AFTER WEAKENING OVER MALAGASY'S
TERRAIN, THE SYSTEM SHOULD REMAIN WEAK AS ITS REMNANTS MOVE OVER THE
NORTHERN MOZAMBIQUE CHANNEL. MODEL SPREAD IS HIGH OVER THE WHOLE
FORECAST'S LENGTH, SO THE INTENSITY FORECAST REMAINS QUITE UNCERTAIN.
