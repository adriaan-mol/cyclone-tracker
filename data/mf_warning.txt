THE TROPICAL DISTURBANCE STAGE.

A MID-TROPOSPHERIC NEAR-EQUATORIAL RIDGE CENTERED OVER THE NORTHERN
PART OF THE BASIN INITIALLY IMPOSES A SOUTHWARD TRACK, WHILE THE
UPPER-LEVEL DYNAMICS ARE WEAK. SUBSEQUENTLY, THE SYSTEM CURVES ITS
PATH SOUTHWESTWARD, CIRCUMNAVIGATING THIS RIDGE TO THE SOUTH AND THEN
BEING STEERED BY THE EXPANSION OF A MID- TO UPPER-TROPOSPHERIC
SUBTROPICAL RIDGE TO THE SOUTH OF THE SYSTEM. IT SHOULD BE NOTED THAT
THERE IS HIGH UNCERTAINTY AMONG THE MODELS REGARDING THE TRACK OF
SYSTEM 03-20262027. SOME MODELS MAINTAIN A SOUTHWARD TRACK FOR
LONGER, LEADING THE SYSTEM INTO A MORE HOSTILE ENVIRONMENT, A
SCENARIO THAT THE RSMC DOES NOT FOLLOW.

REGARDING THE INTENSITY OF THE SYSTEM, THE OCEANIC HEAT CONTENT IS
NOT LIMITING. AN EFFICIENT UPPER-LEVEL OUTFLOW CHANNEL IS OBSERVED ON
THE POLEWARD SIDE AND IS RATHER CORRECT ON THE EQUATORIAL SIDE.
CURRENTLY, THE FACTORS LIMITING INTENSIFICATION ARE: MODERATE
NORTHEASTERLY DEEP-LAYER SHEAR AND THE EFFICIENCY OF THE TRADE WIND
PUSH RELATED TO SYSTEM 01U ORIGINATING FROM THE AUSTRALIAN BASIN,
WHICH DISRUPTS THE LOW-LEVEL VORTICITY. IN THIS CONTEXT, SYSTEM 03
STRUGGLES TO INTENSIFY AS THE CIRCULATION CONSOLIDATES SOMEWHAT UNDER
CONVECTIVE ACTIVITY THAT IS GRADUALLY INCREASING. THE DECREASE IN
DEEP-LAYER SHEAR OVER THE NEXT 12 HOURS, COUPLED WITH THE SYSTEM'S
MOVEMENT TOWARD THE SOUTHWEST, SHOULD ALLOW INTENSIFICATION TO THE
STAGE OF A MODERATE TROPICAL STORM. SUBSEQUENTLY, COMPETITION WITH
ANOTHER SUSPECT AREA TO THE NORTHWEST AND, IN THE LONGER TERM, THE
RESUMPTION OF NORTHWESTERLY DEEP-LAYER SHEAR DUE TO THE
WEST-SOUTHWESTWARD MOVEMENT SHOULD LIMIT THE SYSTEM'S INTENSIFICATION
TO THE STAGE OF A SEVERE TROPICAL STORM.
