ALIGNS WELL WITH OUR SUBJECTIVE DVORAK ANALYSIS IN PT AND DT AT 2.0
WITH A CI OF 2.5.

IN TERMS OF TRACK,  EXCEPT THAT AGUEDA'S WESTWARD MOVEMENT IS
SLIGHTLY SLOWER THAN PREVIOUS FORECAST. AGUEDA CONTINUES TO MOVE
WESTWARD ALONG THE EDGE OF A LOW- TO MID-TROPOSPHERIC RIDGE TO THE
SOUTH OF THE SYSTEM. FROM SATURDAY ONWARDS, AS THE RIDGE EXPANDS OVER
AFRICA, THE LOW-LEVEL STEERING FLOW SHOULD DIRECT THE TRACK TOWARDS
THE WEST-NORTHWEST. THE MODELS ARE IN GOOD AGREEMENT ON THE OVERALL
DIRECTION OF THE SYSTEM'S MOVEMENT, LEADING IT TOWARDS THE AFRICAN
COAST OFF KENYA BY SUNDAY.

IN TERMS OF INTENSITY, THE ENVIRONMENT NEAR AGUEDA REMAINS
UNFAVORABLE, WITH A WELL-ESTABLISHED NORTHERLY SHEAR AND CONTINUING
DRY AIR INTRUSION. DUE TO BURSTS OF CONVECTION, INTENSITY
FLUCTUATIONS REMAIN POSSIBLE, FACILITATED BY THE SMALL SIZE OF THE
SYSTEM. A MORE MARKED FILLING OF THE DEPRESSION WILL OCCUR OVER THE
WEEKEND OFF THE AFRICAN COAST WITHOUT SUSTAINED DEEP CONVECTION.
