PATH IN THE NORTHERN MOZAMBIQUE CHANNEL NEAR CAP D'AMBRE BY TUESDAY.

IN TERMS OF INTENSITY, THE LACK OF SURFACE CONVERGENCE AND MODERATE
SHEAR ARE THE MAIN FACTORS LIMITING ANY POSSIBLE INTENSIFICATION,
DESPITE GOOD UPPER DIVERGENCE POLEWARD. HOWEVER, THE SYSTEM COULD
BENEFIT FROM ITS PASSAGE OVER WATERS WITH HIGHER OCEAN HEAT CONTENT
IN THE COMING HOURS, WHICH COULD ALLOW CONVECTION TO PERSIST NEAR THE
CENTER. EVEN THOUGH THE RSMC FORECAST AND THE MAIN AVAILABLE GUIDANCE
NO LONGER INDICATE THIS, IT IS NOT YET COMPLETELY RULED OUT THAT THE
SYSTEM COULD TEMPORARILY INTENSIFY TO TROPICAL STORM STATE. THE
SYSTEM SHOULD THUS REACH THE FAR NORTH OF MADAGASCAR AS A TROPICAL
DEPRESSION BY SUNDAY EVENING. FROM MONDAY, THE REMAINS OF THE SYSTEM
SHOULD DISSIPATE OVER THE NORTHERN MOZAMBIQUE CHANNEL.
