OVER THE LAST 6 HOURS, CONVECTIVE ACTIVITY REMAINED NEAR THE CENTRE
OF SYSTEM 02. HOWEVER, THE CLOUD PATTERN REMAINS SHEARED (MODERATE
WESTERLY SHEAR) WITH APPARENT BURSTS FURTHER AWAY FROM THE CENTRE. IN
THE ABSENCE OF QUALITATIVE MICROWAVE DATA, THE SYSTEM'S CURRENT
INTENSITY IS UNCERTAIN.

IN TERMS OF TRACK, THE SYSTEM FOLLOWED A WEST-SOUTH-WESTWARD TRACK ON
THE EDGE OF THE LOW-LEVEL SUBTROPICAL RIDGE. FROM TOMORROW, WITH THE
SUBTROPICAL RIDGE STRENGTHENING SOUTH OF MADAGASCAR, THE SYSTEM IS
EXPECTED TO GRADUALLY TURN NORTH-WESTWARD. GIVEN THIS TRACK, THE
SYSTEM COULD MAKE LANDFALL ON THE FAR NORTH OF MADAGASCAR BY MONDAY.
HOWEVER, THERE IS A SIGNIFICANT SPREAD BETWEEN THE MODELS, WHICH ARE
STRUGGLING TO ANALYSE THE CENTRE OF THIS CIRCULATION ACCURATELY. THIS
SCENARIO THEREFORE REMAINS UNCERTAIN. THE SYSTEM COULD RETURN OVER
SEA, NORTH OF THE MOZAMBIQUE CHANNEL NEAR CAP DA  AMBRE BY TUESDAY.

IN TERMS OF INTENSITY, THE LACK OF SURFACE CONVERGENCE AND MODERATE
SHEAR ARE THE MAIN FACTORS LIMITING ANY POSSIBLE INTENSIFICATION,
DESPITE GOOD UPPER DIVERGENCE POLEWARD. HOWEVER, THE SYSTEM COULD
BENEFIT FROM MOVING OVER MORE ENERGETIC WATERS NORTH OF TROMELIN
TODAY, WHICH COULD ALLOW DEEP CONVECTION PERSISTING NEAR THE CENTRE.
THE SYSTEM COULD THEREFORE TEMPORARILY INTENSIFY TO THE TROPICAL
STORM STAGE WITHIN THE NEXT 24HOURS. STILL THIS SCENARIO IS NOW
RATHER ISOLATED AMONGST THE VARIOUS FORECAST MODELS AVAILABLE. THE
SYSTEM IS THEREFORE EXPECTED TO APPROACH THE NORTHERN TIP OF
MADAGASCAR AS A DEPRESSION OR TROPICAL STORM BY SUNDAY EVENING. FROM
MONDAY, FOLLOWING A WEAKENING CAUSED BY THE MALAGASY LANDMASS, THE
REMAINS OF THE SYSTEM ARE EXPECTED TO DISSIPATE NORTH OF THE
MOZAMBIQUE CHANNEL.
