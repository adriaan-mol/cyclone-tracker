2.C ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:
OVER THE LAST 6 HOURS, INITIALLY-WEAK CONVECTION HAS THEN INCREASED
BEFORE 12Z TO THE SOUTHEAST OF THE SYSTEM, ALTHOUGH REMAINING OFFSET
MORE THAN 35-40 NM AWAY FROM A PARTIALLY EXPOSED CENTRE, PROBABLY
UNDER THE EFFECT OF INCREASING NORTHWESTERLY WIND SHEAR. THIS CENTRE
APPEARS TO BE QUITE TIGHT AND WELL-DEFINED, AS SHOWN BY VISIBLE
SATELLITE AND RECENT MICROWAVE IMAGES (GPM/0537Z, GOSAT-GW/1000Z).
FOLLOWING THIS MORNING'S ASCAT DATA AND GIVEN ACTIVE DEEP CONVECTION
SOUTHEAST OF THE CIRCULATION, MAXIMUM WINDS ARE ESTIMATED AT 30 KT IN
THE SOUTHEASTERN QUADRANT. THE SYSTEM REMAINS A ZONE OF DISTURBED
WEATHER BECAUSE OF ITS LACK OF CONVECTIVE ORGANIZATION.

IN TERMS OF TRACK, THE SYSTEM HAS SHIFTED A BIT MORE WESTWARD THAN
INITIALLY EXPECTED AND SEEMS TO BE SLOWING DOWN A BIT. IT COULD
RESUME ITS SOUTHWESTWARD MOVEMENT BY TOMORROW MORNING IF THE CENTRE
MOVES CLOSER OR UNDER THE CONVECTIVE MASS, WHICH REMAINS QUITE
UNCERTAIN. FROM SATURDAY AFTERNOON, THE TRACK SHOULD HEAD WESTWARD TO
WEST-NORTHWESTWARD UNDER THE INFLUENCE OF A NEW SUBTROPICAL RIDGE
SOUTH OF MADAGASCAR. MODEL SPREAD REMAINS FAIRLY HIGH AS THE TRACK
ALSO DEPENDS ON THE INTENSITY FORECAST.

IN TERMS OF INTENSITY, ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS ARE MIXED, WITH SEA
SURFACE WATERS AROUND 27C, GOOD UPPER DIVERGENCE BUT GRADUALLY
INCREASING WIND SHEAR, AS WELL AS A LACK OF SURFACE CONVERGENCE NORTH
OF SYSTEM. FROM SATURDAY, NORTHWESTERLY WIND SHEAR SHOULD KEEP
INCREASING, ALTHOUGH THE SYSTEM IS EXPECTED TO MOVE OVER SLIGHTLY
WARMER SURFACE WATERS NORTH OF TROMELIN. CONSEQUENTLY THE SYSTEM HAS
A POSSIBILITY TO REACH TROPICAL STORM STAGE BY TOMORROW. THE EFFECTS
OF WIND SHEAR COULD TEMPORARILY INCREASE BY SUNDAY MORNING AS THE
SYSTEM ACCELERATES IN THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION OF WIND SHEAR, WHICH
COULD LEAD TO SOME SLIGHT WEAKENING. ON SUNDAY EVENING, WIND SHEAR
SHOULD DECREASE AGAIN AND LOW-LEVEL CONVERGENCE SHOULD INCREASE AS
THE LOW APPROACHES MADAGASCAR'S COAST. SOME MODELS THUS SUGGEST
POSSIBLE REINTENSIFICATION JUST BEFORE LANDFALL SUNDAY EVENING. FROM
MONDAY ONWARDS, AFTER WEAKENING OVER MALAGASY'S TERRAIN, THE SYSTEM
SHOULD REMAIN WEAK AS ITS REMNANTS MOVE OVER THE NORTHERN MOZAMBIQUE
CHANNEL. MODEL SPREAD IS HIGH OVER THE WHOLE FORECAST'S LENGTH, SO
THE INTENSITY FORECAST REMAINS QUITE UNCERTAIN.
