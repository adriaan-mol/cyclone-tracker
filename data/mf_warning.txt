2.C ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:
OVER THE PAST 6 HOURS, THE SURFACE CENTER HAS REMAINED EXPOSED, WITH
CONVECTION OCCURING FAR FROM THE CENTER IN THE EASTERN SEMICIRCLE.
THE 1449Z WSFM MICROWAVE SWATH ALLOWS US TO ESTIMATE THE CENTER'S
POSITION, WHICH IS CONSISTENT WITH THIS AFTERNOON'S VISIBLE IMAGERY
SHOWING A WEST-NORTHWESTWARD SHIFT IN THE TRACK. DUE TO THE DECREASE
OF CONVECTIVE ORGANIZATION, THE FINAL T-NUMBER IS ESTIMATED AT 1.0.
WITHOUT ANY RECENT ASCAT SWATHS NEAR THE SURFACE CENTER, MAXIMUM
WINDS ESTIMATES ARE LOWERED TO 25 KT.

IN TERMS OF TRACK, THE SYSTEM HAS SHIFTED A BIT MORE WESTWARD THAN
INITIALLY EXPECTED AND SEEMS TO BE SLOWING DOWN A BIT. IT COULD
RESUME ITS SOUTHWESTWARD MOVEMENT BY TOMORROW MORNING IF THE CENTRE
MOVES CLOSER OR UNDER THE CONVECTIVE MASS, WHICH REMAINS QUITE
UNCERTAIN. FROM SATURDAY AFTERNOON, THE TRACK SHOULD HEAD WESTWARD TO
WEST-NORTHWESTWARD UNDER THE INFLUENCE OF A NEW SUBTROPICAL RIDGE
SOUTH OF MADAGASCAR. WITH THIS TRAJECTORY, THE SYSTEM IS EXPECTED TO
MAKE LANDFALL IN THE FAR NORTH OF MADAGASCAR BY SUNDAY EVENING.
HOWEVER, THERE IS SIGNIFICANT SPREAD AMONG THE MODELS REGARDING THE
LANDFALL AREA DUE TO A TRAJECTORY THAT DEPENDS ON THE SYSTEMA  S
INTENSITY. THE SYSTEM IS EXPECTED TO MOVE BACK OUT TO SEA IN THE
NORTH OF THE MOZAMBIQUE CHANNEL ON MONDAY, MOST LIKELY AS AN AREA OF
DISTURBED WEATHER.

IN TERMS OF INTENSITY, ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS ARE MIXED, WITH
RATHER COOL SEA SURFACE WATERS AROUND 26 TO 27C, GOOD UPPER
DIVERGENCE BUT GRADUALLY INCREASING WIND SHEAR, AS WELL AS A LACK OF
SURFACE CONVERGENCE NORTH OF SYSTEM. FROM SATURDAY, NORTHWESTERLY
WIND SHEAR SHOULD KEEP INCREASING, ALTHOUGH THE SYSTEM IS EXPECTED TO
MOVE OVER SLIGHTLY WARMER SURFACE WATERS NORTH OF TROMELIN.
CONSEQUENTLY THE SYSTEM HAS A POSSIBILITY TO REACH TROPICAL STORM
STAGE BY TOMORROW. THE EFFECTS OF WIND SHEAR COULD TEMPORARILY
INCREASE BY SUNDAY MORNING AS THE SYSTEM ACCELERATES IN THE OPPOSITE
DIRECTION OF WIND SHEAR, WHICH COULD LEAD TO SOME SLIGHT WEAKENING.
ON SUNDAY EVENING, WIND SHEAR SHOULD DECREASE AGAIN AND LOW-LEVEL
CONVERGENCE SHOULD INCREASE AS THE LOW APPROACHES MADAGASCAR'S COAST.
SOME MODELS THUS SUGGEST POSSIBLE REINTENSIFICATION JUST BEFORE
LANDFALL SUNDAY EVENING. FROM MONDAY ONWARDS, AFTER WEAKENING OVER
MALAGASY'S TERRAIN, THE SYSTEM SHOULD REMAIN WEAK AS ITS REMNANTS
MOVE OVER THE NORTHERN MOZAMBIQUE CHANNEL. MODEL SPREAD IS HIGH OVER
THE WHOLE FORECAST'S LENGTH, SO THE INTENSITY FORECAST REMAINS QUITE
UNCERTAIN.
