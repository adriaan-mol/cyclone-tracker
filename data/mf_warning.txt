OVER THE LAST 6 HOURS, CONVECTIVE ACTIVITY HAS RESUMED NEAR THE
CENTRE OF SYSTEM 02. HOWEVER, THE CLOUD PATTERN REMAINS SHEARED
(MODERATE WESTERLY SHEAR) WITH BURSTS OF CONVECTION CONFINED TO THE
SOUTHEASTERN QUADRANT. MICROWAVE IMAGES (0200Z WSFM AND 0333Z F16)
SHOW A SLIGHT CONSOLIDATION OF THE INNER CIRCULATION, WITH A MORE
SYMMETRICAL CORE. A PARTIAL ASCAT PASS ALSO INDICATES NEAR GALE FORCE
WINDS IN THE SOUTHEASTERN QUADRANT. THE SYSTEM IS THEREFORE
RECLASSIFIED AS TROPICAL DEPRESSION 30K AT 06Z.

IN TERMS OF TRACK, THE SYSTEM IS EXPECTED TO MOVE WEST-SOUTH-WESTWARD
OVER THE NEXT 12 HOURS IN LINE WITH THE LOW-LEVEL SUBTROPICAL RIDGE.
FROM TOMORROW, WITH THE SUBTROPICAL RIDGE STRENGTHENING SOUTH OF
MADAGASCAR, THE SYSTEM IS EXPECTED TO GRADUALLY TURN NORTH-WEST.
GIVEN THIS TRACK, THE SYSTEM COULD MAKE LANDFALL ON THE FAR NORTH OF
MADAGASCAR BY MONDAY. HOWEVER, THERE IS A SIGNIFICANT SPREAD BETWEEN
THE MODELS, WHICH ARE STRUGGLING TO LOCATE THE CENTRE OF THIS
CIRCULATION ACCURATELY. THIS SCENARIO THEREFORE REMAINS UNCERTAIN.
THE SYSTEM COULD RETURN OVER SEA, NORTH OF THE MOZAMBIQUE CHANNEL
NEAR CAP DA  AMBRE BY TUESDAY.

IN TERMS OF INTENSITY, THE LACK OF SURFACE CONVERGENCE AND MODERATE
SHEAR ARE THE MAIN FACTORS LIMITING ANY POSSIBLE INTENSIFICATION,
DESPITE GOOD UPPER DIVERGENCE. HOWEVER, THE SYSTEM COULD BENEFIT FROM
MOVING OVER MORE ENERGETIC WATERS NORTH OF TROMELIN TODAY, WHICH
COULD ALLOW DEEP CONVECTION PERSISTING NEAR THE CENTRE;  THE SYSTEM
COULD THEREFORE TEMPORARILY INTENSIFY TO THE TROPICAL STORM STAGE
WITHIN THE NEXT 24 TO 48 HOURS. STILL THIS SCENARIO IS NOW RATHER
ISOLATED AMONGST THE VARIOUS FORECAST MODELS AVAILABLE. THE SYSTEM IS
THEREFORE EXPECTED TO APPROACH THE NORTHERN TIP OF MADAGASCAR AS A
DEPRESSION OR TROPICAL STORM BY SUNDAY EVENING. FROM MONDAY,
FOLLOWING A WEAKENING CAUSED BY THE MALAGASY LANDMASS, THE REMAINS OF
THE SYSTEM ARE EXPECTED TO DISSIPATE NORTH OF THE MOZAMBIQUE CHANNEL.
