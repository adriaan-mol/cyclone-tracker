IN TERMS OF TRACK, THE SYSTEM FOLLOWED A WEST TRACK ON THE EDGE OF
THE LOW-LEVEL SUBTROPICAL RIDGE LOCATED BETWEEN MADAGASCAR AND
MASCARENES ISLANDS. FROM TOMORROW, WITH THE SUBTROPICAL RIDGE
STRENGTHENING OVER MADAGASCAR AND AS THE SYSTEM WEAKENS AND IS
STEERED BY THE TRADE WINDS, IT SHOULD GRADUALLY MOVE TOWARD THE
NORTHWEST. REGARDING THIS TRACK, THE SYSTEM COULD SKIM THE FAR NORTH
OF MADAGASCAR BY SUNDAY NIGHT INTO MONDAY. THE SPREAD AMONG THE MAIN
MODELS HAS DECREASED, BUT THEY STILL STRUGGLE TO CORRECTLY ANALYZE
THE CENTER OF THIS CIRCULATION AND ITS WEAKENING. THIS SCENARIO
THEREFORE REMAINS TO BE CONFIRMED. THE SYSTEM COULD RE-EMERGE OVER
THE SEA IN THE NORTHERN MOZAMBIQUE CHANNEL NEAR CAP D'AMBRE BY
TUESDAY.

IN TERMS OF INTENSITY, THE LACK OF SURFACE CONVERGENCE AND MODERATE
SHEAR ARE THE MAIN FACTORS LIMITING ANY POSSIBLE INTENSIFICATION,
DESPITE GOOD UPPER DIVERGENCE POLEWARD. HOWEVER, THE SYSTEM COULD
BENEFIT FROM ITS PASSAGE OVER WATERS WITH HIGHER OCEAN HEAT CONTENT
IN THE COMING HOURS, WHICH COULD ALLOW CONVECTION TO PERSIST NEAR THE
CENTER. EVEN THOUGH THE RSMC FORECAST AND THE MAIN AVAILABLE GUIDANCE
NO LONGER INDICATE THIS, IT IS NOT YET COMPLETELY RULED OUT THAT THE
SYSTEM COULD TEMPORARILY INTENSIFY TO TROPICAL STORM STATE BY THE END
OF THE NIGHT. THE SYSTEM SHOULD THUS REACH THE FAR NORTH OF
MADAGASCAR AS A TROPICAL DEPRESSION BY SUNDAY EVENING. FROM MONDAY,
THE REMAINS OF THE SYSTEM SHOULD DISSIPATE OVER THE NORTHERN
MOZAMBIQUE CHANNEL.
