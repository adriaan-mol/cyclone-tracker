PART 2 : TROPICAL WEATHER DISCUSSION:

As Tropical Depression No. 02-20262027 moves southwestward, the basin no longer contains any significant features. Convective activity is strong within 02-20262027, as monitored by the CMRS, and moderate in the far northeast of the basin, within a zone of trade wind convergence.

For the next 5 days, over the western side of the basin, the vorticity and moisture ccontent from the Rossby wave associated with the currently monitored low-pressure system will gradually shift westward and then move away. Over the eastern side of the basin, the equatorial easterly wind anomalies (influenced by El Nino and a weak positive IOD) continue to create unfavorable conditions for cyclogenesis. However, early next week, the passage of a Kelvin wave in the North Indian Ocean, coupled with the regeneration of the MJO, could lead to the formation of a Near-Equatorial Trough (NET) over the eastern side of the basin.
