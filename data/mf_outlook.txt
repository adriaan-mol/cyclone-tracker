PART 2 : TROPICAL WEATHER DISCUSSION:

The basin displays a Monsoon Trough (MT) pattern between 55 and 80oE along 5 to 8oS. Convective activity is moderate to strong within the MT, particularly in its eastern part near a tropical disturbance east of Diego Garcia, for which the RSMC will be issuing bulletins from 12 UTC onwards (system number 03-20262027). Convective activity is also present near the remnants of system 01U over the eastern part of the basin.

Equatorial wave activity remains mostly conducive for cyclogenesis over the central and eastern parts of the basin in the short term. The westerly wind surge in the wake of the moist MJO is still ongoing over the extreme east of the basin but will move away by the end of the week as the MJO's dry phase sets in. The prevailing contribution comes from an Equatorial Rossby wave which strongly enhances northwesterly winds over a large central part of the basin, thus increasing convergence on the northern side of the MT. Besides, mixed Rossby-gravity wave activity remains favorable until Wednesday, then should become more unfavorable or neutral at the end of the week.

Remnant low over the east of the basin :System 01U, previously monitored by the BOM, is currently over the east of the basin. Satellite imagery shows a well-defined cloud vortex in the southern part of a low-level trough, with a centre estimated near 14.5oS/85.5oE. Estimated winds are very asymmetrical, reaching near 25 kt in the southern semi-circle in the pressure gradient and due to convection, but only near 10 kt in its northern part. Strong westerly to northwesterly wind shear maintains convection displaced to the southeast of the vortex, limiting odds of reintensification. This low should keep moving westward while gradually filling in the coming days.

For the next 5 days, there is no potential for this suspect area to develop into a tropical storm.

Low-pressure area to the west of Diego-Garcia :A broad area of low-pressure is located within the western half of the MT, west of Diego-Garcia, with a centre located near 6oS/67oE this Monday. This morning's ASCAT passes do not show a well-defined circulation. Convection is moderate to the northeast of the low, without particular organisation. This area should benefit from good low-level inflow from the monsoon flow to its north, as wind shear remains low to moderate, and with good upper divergence and high oceanic heat content. However, cyclogenesis potential could suffer from a lack of convergence on its southern side, especially due to the presence of system no03-20262027 to its south then southeast.Models mostly suggest a low cyclogenesis potential, or up to moderate according to the EPS. Some rare deterministic model runs suggest tropical storm development by the end of the week.

The risk of tropical storm development to the west or southwest of Diego-Garcia is expected to be low from Friday November 27th.



10-day outlook:
During early December, despite the dry phase of the MJO, a new crossing between a Kelvin wave and a new equatorial Rossby wave at the eastern edge of the basin could enhance equatorial westerlies and enable the formation of a new low-pressure area near 90oE or in the Indonesian area of responsibility. According to some models, this low could then move west of 90oE while more or less developing.
