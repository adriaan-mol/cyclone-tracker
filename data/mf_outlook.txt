PART 2 : TROPICAL WEATHER DISCUSSION:

The basin exhibits a Monsoon Trough (MT) pattern between 55 and 80oE along 5 to 10oS. Convective activity is moderate to strong within the MT, particularly in its eastern portion near tropical disturbance 03-20262027 southeast of Diego Garcia, but also further west around another suspect area and, to a lesser extent, around the remnants of Australian system 01U.

The wave environment remains generally favorable for cyclogenesis over the central and eastern parts of the basin in the short term. Indeed, the tail of the westerly wind burst associated with the active MJO, the powerful equatorial Rossby wave, and the mixed Rossby-gravity wave activity have contributed to the persistence of the Monsoon Trough.

Remnant low over the east of the basin :System 01U, previously monitored by the BOM, continues to move across the eastern part of the basin, with an estimated center at approximately 16.9oS/83.4oE at 10Z. The 0403Z ASCAT-C pass shows that the estimated associated winds are highly asymmetrical, reaching 25 to 30 kt in the southern semicircle due to the pressure gradient, while much weaker on the northern side. Strong westerly to northwesterly wind shear is keeping convection offset to the southeast of the vortex. This system will continue moving westward while gradually filling in over the next few days.

For the next 5 days, there is no potential for this suspect area to develop into a tropical storm.

Suspect area west of Diego-Garcia :A low-pressure system is still present in the western half of the MT, currently centered approximately near 7.1oS/68.6oE, west of Diego Garcia. The latest satellite data (classical and microwave imagery) show increased signs of curvature. However, the cloud tops remain fairly warm. The latest partial ASCAT passes do not appear to show winds exceeding 25/30kt.Over the next two days, this area will benefit from a strong equatorial feed in the lower levels, with wind shear expected to remain rather weak to moderate, along with good upper-level divergence and strong oceanic potential. The most limiting factor is likely a lack of convergence on the southern flank, particularly due to the presence of system 03-20262027 to the southeast. Starting Friday and especially Saturday, the gradual strengthening of westerly to northwesterly wind shear is expected to lead to a weakening of the system.A generally weak-to-moderate cyclogenesis signal is observed across the various ensembles, with the AI models lagging slightly behind the physical models.

The risk of tropical storm development to the southwest of the Chagos archipelago is expected to be low from tomorrow Wednesday November 25th and moderate from Thursday November 26th.



10-day outlook:
During early December, despite the dry phase of the MJO, a new crossing between a Kelvin wave and a new equatorial Rossby wave at the eastern edge of the basin could enhance equatorial westerlies and enable the formation of a new low-pressure area near 90oE or in the Indonesian area of responsibility. According to some models, this low could then move west of 90oE while more or less developing.
