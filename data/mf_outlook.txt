PART 2 : TROPICAL WEATHER DISCUSSION:

The basin displays a Monsoon Trough (MT) pattern between 55 and 80oE along 5 to 7oS. Convective activity is moderate to strong within the MT, particularly in its eastern part near a suspect area east of Diego Garcia. Convective activity is also present near the remnants of system 01U, which was previously monitored by the BOM and has re-entered our area of responsibility.

Overall, conditions remain favorable for cyclogenesis over the central and eastern parts of the basin in the short term, with a still-pronounced westward wind surge persisting behind the MJO's active phase, reinforced by a powerful Equatorial Rossby wave and significant mixed Rossby-Gravity activity.

Remnant low, east of the basin :System 01U, previously monitored by the BOM, has re-entered our area of responsibility this morning. However, the 0303Z ASCAT-C pass confirms the absence of a well-defined center, with an elongated circulation. Maximum winds still reached 30 kt in the southern semicircle. Convective activity still present in the eastern semicircle has, over the past few hours, begun to decouple from the maximum vorticity due to deep wind shear. In all likelihood, this system will continue moving west-southwest while weakening over the next few days.

For the next 5 days, there is no potential for this suspect area to develop into a moderate tropical storm.

Suspect area, east of Diego-Garcia :The latest observations, notably the 0442Z ASCAT-C pass, show the presence of a still elongated but closed broad surface circulation, located near 6.7S / 76.5E around 10Z. Maximum winds exceed 25 kt.This system will continue to evolve over the next few days in an environment conducive to its development, with a good equatorial feed, within a zone of low wind shear and good upper-level divergenceparticularly on the polar sideand strong oceanic potential. The most limiting factor remains the polar side low-level convergence, which could be hindered by the presence of the remnants of the system to the east.In this environment, the models show an increasing signal of cyclogenesis, ranging from weak to moderate for the IA ensemble models (possibly due to the system's size) to high for the EPS and GEFS models over the next few days. The latest deterministic models (IFS, HAFS, HWRF) also suggest that this system will reach the stage of a moderate tropical storm as early as Monday to Tuesday night or Tuesday daytime.

The likelihood of the formation of a moderate tropical storm becomes moderate tomorrow Monday November 23th and high from Tuesday 24th.
