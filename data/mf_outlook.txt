PART 2 : TROPICAL WEATHER DISCUSSION:

The basin has a double Monsoon Trough (MT) pattern, with one branch between 48oE and 65oE extending from west of the Seychelles to the area of disturbed weather described below, and another branch east of this area extending up to 75oE. The strongest convective activity is located around this disturbed area, but moderate to strong activity is also found in the eastern MT branch.

The center of the basin will maintain favorable conditions for cyclogenesis over the next five days due to the passage of a Rossby wave combined with the brief contribution of a Mixed Rossby-Gravity (MRG) wave by Sunday. Further east, the dry phase of the Rossby wave complicates conditions for cyclogenesis. Further west, the emergence of the dry phase of the MJO is the limiting factor.

Area of disturbed weather to the west of Diego-Garcia :A low-pressure system is still present in the middle of the basin. The ASCAT-C data at 0440 UTC reveals a highly elongated center with maximum winds around 20 kt and a center near 9.68oS/66.0oE.Until tomorrow; this area will benefit from strong equatorial low-level inflow with a weak to moderate wind shear, a good upper-level divergence and a high oceanic potential.The most limiting factor is likely a lack of convergence on the southern side, particularly due to the presence of remnant system 03-20262027 to the southeast. However, Today and especially tomorrow, the wave environment will help strengthen convergence by enhancing the northerly flow, thus improving conditions for cyclogenesis. From Sunday onward, the gradual increase in west-to-northwest wind shear, the inflow of dry air, and decreasing oceanic potential should lead to a weakening of the system.The deterministic models, except for the high-resolution AROME model, do not forecast deepening to tropical storm stage, while the ensemble models suggest a low risk for tomorrow and Sunday morning southwest of Diego Garcia. The AI models, on the other hand, struggle to reach tropical storm stage.

The risk of tropical storm formation southwest of the Chagos is low the Saturday, November 28th, through Sunday, November 29th.
