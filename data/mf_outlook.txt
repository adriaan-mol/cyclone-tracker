PART 2 : TROPICAL WEATHER DISCUSSION:

A monsoon trough (MT) structure persists between 60oE and 80oE near 7oS. Convective activity is moderate to strong on both sides of the MT and in the southern semicircle of the depression minimum 01U, tracked by the Perth TCWC, located in the Australian area of responsibility near 11.6oS/91oE at 06 UTC.

The wave pattern remains generally favorable for cyclogenesis over the central and eastern parts of the basin, with a well-marked westerly wind surge behind the active phase of the MJO. This contributes to maintaining favorable conditions for the persistence of the MT structure over the central basin, within which low-level vorticity will consolidate.

Area of disturbed weather near 90oE in the Australian area of responsibility:Monitored by the BOM, system 01U can be classified as an area of disturbed weather according to our basin's classification, with maximum winds of around 30/35 kt, following BOM's analysis. According to BOM forecasts, the system will re-enter our area of responsibility by Sunday, 22nd, shortly after 00 UTC. The system is expected to continue on a southward track over the next 12 hours, before turning west-southwestward under the influence of a weak low-level ridge transiting further south. According to their forecast, the system is expected to gradually weaken as it moves into our area, transitioning to a remnant low. In this context, the system will not be tracked by the RSMC of Reunion unless it reintensifies.

The risk of a tropical storm developing over the far eastern part of the basin is low over the next 48 hours.

Area of interest near the Chagos Archipelago:Within the MT structure defined between 60oE and 80oE along 7oS, scatterometer data indicate a clearer development of low-level vorticity near 76oE/7oS. Both deterministic and ensemble models suggest that this circulation could intensify into a tropical storm within the MT by mid-next week.

The likelihood of the formation of a moderate tropical storm remains low from tuesday november 24th.
